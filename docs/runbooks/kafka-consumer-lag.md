# Kafka Consumer Lag

Identify group/topic/partition and compare arrival rate to processing capacity. Inspect poison messages, database latency and rebalances. Scale only stateless consumers, preserve partition-key ordering, and watch inbox/DLQ growth. Do not reset offsets without a reviewed replay position.

Every action must record operator, UTC timestamps, environment, affected service/version, evidence, rollback decision, and final verification. Never expose secrets or delete source data during incident response.
